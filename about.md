## Our mission

We partner with communities to meet their basic human needs through sustainable engineering projects, while giving students real-world experience in design, teamwork and leadership.

## What we do

- **Community projects.** We take on multi-year projects with partner communities, covering water supply, sanitation, energy and structures.
- **Local outreach.** We run STEM outreach and service projects close to home.
- **Professional growth.** Members get technical mentoring, practice writing design reports, and attend regional and national conferences.

## How we work

Every project follows a full engineering cycle: assessment, design, implementation and monitoring. Each one is done *with* the community, not *for* it. Students lead the work, and professional mentors and faculty advisors back us up.
